This document maps **pillar clusters** to real CinNova businesses and destinations. Supporting articles are **concepts / ideas only** — not a mass-generation brief and not a claim that those URLs already exist.

## Cross-cluster linking rules

1. **News → Evergreen:** Time-sensitive `/news/:slug` should link to durable Blog/Guides/Product pages where relevant.
2. **Evergreen → Money pages:** Blog/Guides should deep-link to one primary commercial destination (Book, Product, Calculator, or Newsletter) — not a footer of every CTA.
3. **Status honesty:** AVAILABLE / COMING_SOON / IN_DEVELOPMENT / product build statuses must match catalog data.
4. **No volume theater:** Do not prioritize clusters by invented keyword volumes; prioritize fit to shipped surfaces and monetization funnel.
5. **Funnel reminder:** organic → content → Books/Products/Newsletter → Amazon outbound / lead.
